THE PLATFORM

Build better structures.
Ask better questions.

One focused workspace for taking an idea beyond how it looks, toward how it behaves. Designed to make structural decisions clearer, faster, and more defensible.

01 / EXPLORE

See the structure

Inspect forces, stress, displacement, and supports directly on an interactive truss model.

02 / OPTIMIZE

Find stronger choices

Search for lighter feasible designs while respecting critical engineering constraints.

03 / CHALLENGE

Push the limits

Increase loads, reduce material performance, and discover where a design stops working.

04 / VERIFY

Trust, then verify

Use ML to suggest candidates, then solve the real physics again before accepting them.

WHY WE BUILT IT

AI should expand what engineers can explore. Not replace what engineers need to prove.

ForceForge AI is a student-built engineering prototype created for Hack the Nest 2026 at Eastern Michigan University. We connect intuitive design exploration with finite element checks, optimization, and AI-assisted discovery in a single browser experience.
